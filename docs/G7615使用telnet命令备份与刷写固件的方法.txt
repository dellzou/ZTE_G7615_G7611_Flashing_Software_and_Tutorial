G7615使用telnet命令备份与刷写固件的方法

分区备份命令,根据实际情况更改USB2_disc1
nand kread /dev/mtd1 0x0 0x300000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd1.bin         //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd2 0x0 0x200000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd2.bin        //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd3 0x0 0x400000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd3.bin        //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd4 0x0 0x400000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd4.bin        //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd5 0x0 0x800000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd5.bin        //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd7 0x0 0x1000000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd7.bin       //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd10 0x0 0x3000000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd10.bin    //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd12 0x0 0x2ac0000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd12.bin    //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin
nand kread /dev/mtd13 0x0 0x2800000 /mnt/USB2_disc1/mtd13.bin    //备份mtd1到u盘mtd1.bin


upgradetest switchver 0   //切换版本0或1
fw_flashing  //查看当前主备分区调用情况
cat /proc/mtd   //查看全部分区
cat /proc/csp/versionstates   //查看分区激活及校验信息

fw_flashing                                                                            //查看当前调用分区情况，0在mtd9分区,1在mtd10分区
cd /mnt/USB2_disc1                                                               //进入u盘目录,根据实际情况更改USB2_disc1
echo 2 > /proc/zteinfo/factory/factorymode                             //写入数字2到factorymode
touch /userconfig/nanddebug                                                 //创建nanddubug临时目录
nand kerase /dev/mtd7 0x0 0x1000000                                   //擦除mtd7
nand kwrite /dev/mtd7 0x0 0x1000000 ./mtd7.bin                    //写入mtd7
nand kerase /dev/mtd10 0x0 0x3000000                                //擦除mtd10
nand kwrite /dev/mtd10 0x0 0x3000000 ./mtd10.bin                //写入mtd10
nand kerase /dev/mtd8 0x0 0x200000                                   //擦除mtd8 dbus分区
nand kerase /dev/mtd11 0x0 0x6800000                                //擦除mtd11 plugin_data分区
rm /userconfig/nanddebug                                                   //删除nanddebug临时目录
reboot                                                                               //重启光猫

如果使用fw_flashing查看到正在使用的分区,0就代表正在使用mtd6/mtd9,所要写入和擦除的就必须为mtd7/mtd10,在完成擦写备份分区后使用upgradetest switchver 1   切换主备分区,然后再刷写mtd6/mtd9.
